### Provenance of the figures

The published Clause 40 and Clause 55 of IEEE Std 802.3 were not read; the design owner accepted the working-group text as final on 2026-11-16 and closed the published-text question as accepted `[decision]`.

### Which figure governs

For the medianet's GbE default links, 1000BASE-T governs, because gigabit copper default links are 1000BASE-T and the bit error ratio of the PHY in use sets the guaranteed worst case `[decision]`. Where the 10 Gb/s links of C-25 are 10GBASE-T copper, the 10GBASE-T figure applies to them; C-25 does not name the PHY `[decision]`.

A standard's bit error ratio is a bound on a compliant link, not the error rate of a typical clean link: the task force's response says "The expected BER will be significantly better" `[standard]`. A measured clean-network loss well below the 1e-10 floor is therefore expected and is not a contradiction; L1 (task 019) measures it `[decision]`.

Design-owner decisions of 2026-11-16 (given after the first review) `[decision]`: (1) C-90 states both figures, labelled: the 1000BASE-T objective of 1e-10 as the guaranteed worst case, and the design assumption of 1e-12 as what a clean link is expected to meet, pending L1; (2) C-118 predicts about 58 losses at the assumption and at most about 5 800 at the objective; (3) the C-179 reopen level stays about 580 losses in the 24-hour soak, decoupled from the published figure, an absolute level and no longer 10 × the published floor, so a compliant-but-poor link at 1e-10 (about 5 800 losses) does reopen the redundancy decision; (4) ADR 04 stands for v1 with its premise reworded (the standard guarantees only 1e-10 (worst case); the design assumes links at 1e-12 or better, to be measured by the L1 soak), and its falsifier "the published bit-error figure differs materially" is recorded as met on 2026-11-16 and answered by this decision.

### Loss-floor recomputation

Inputs: the default packet is 1 320 bytes on the wire (C-20), preamble, inter-frame gap and FCS included, which is 10 560 bits `[decision]`. The per-stream rate is 2 000 packets/s. The soak runs 64 000 packets/s for 86 400 s (24 h), about 5.5e9 packets (C-118). Formulas: p = BER × 10 560; mean time between losses = 1 / (p × 2 000) s; soak count = p × 64 000 × 86 400; the last column is 10 × soak count, the superseded C-179 rule, kept only to show what it would have given `[decision]`.

| BER | Per-packet loss probability p | Mean hours between losses per stream per link | C-118 soak losses | Reopen count under the superseded 10 × soak rule (decided level stays about 580) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-10 | 1.1e-6 | 0.13 | 5.8e3 | 5.8e4 | [standard] |
| 1e-12 | 1.1e-8 | 13 | 58 | 580 | [standard] |

The 1e-10 row is the governing row for the GbE default links (1000BASE-T), the guaranteed worst case; the 1e-12 row is the 10GBASE-T figure and also the design assumption for a clean GbE link, kept by decision. At 1e-10 the mean time between losses is about 473 s, 7.9 min, per stream per link `[standard]`.

Arithmetic, recomputed `[decision]`. Wire packet 1 320 bytes x 8 = 10 560 bits. At BER 1e-10: p = 1e-10 x 10 560 = 1.056e-6, about 1.1e-6; losses per second = 1.056e-6 x 2 000 = 2.112e-3, so one loss every 473.5 s = 0.1315 h, about 0.13 h; soak = 1.056e-6 x 64 000 x 86 400 = 5 839.3, about 5.8e3 (soak packets 64 000 x 86 400 = 5.53e9, about 5.5e9); 10 x soak = 58 393, about 5.8e4. At BER 1e-12: p = 1.056e-8, about 1.1e-8; one loss every 47 348 s = 13.15 h, about 13 h; soak = 58.39, about 58; 10 x soak = 583.9, about 580.

### Verdict on C-90

**verdict:** C-90's published figure is corrected for 1000BASE-T, because the guaranteed bit error ratio is 1e-10 and not 1e-12: p 1.1e-6 per default packet, one loss per stream per link about every 0.13 h (473 s), a C-118 soak prediction of at most 5.8e3, and 5.8e4 as 10 × soak at that figure, a level the decision does not adopt (C-179 stays about 580); the design assumption of 1e-12 (p 1.1e-8, 13 h, 58) is kept by decision `[standard]`

### Open sub-question

The open sub-question "Is the loss floor C-90 computed from the right bit-error figure?" is answered: C-90 used 1e-12, which is the 10GBASE-T figure and the design assumption, while the 1000BASE-T figure is 1e-10 (working-group text, see above), so the published figure is corrected and both figures are stated; the clean-network measurement is handed to L1 (task 019) `[decision]`. Owner of the measurement: L1 task 019. Falsifier of the 1e-12 assumption: the clean-wire loss measured in the L1 24-hour soak is above about 580 losses (C-179; not yet measured, so not met) `[decision]`.
